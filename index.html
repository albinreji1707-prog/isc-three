OUR HERITAGE

Home Away From Home Since 1967

A historical legacy built on community, culture, and deep friendship between India and the UAE.

India Social & Cultural Centre (ISC), Abu Dhabi

India Social Cultural Centre (ISC), Abu Dhabi, is the premier socio-cultural organisation and the apex body of the registered Indian associations in the capital city of Abu Dhabi. The genesis of ISC is traced to the Unity Club that took shape in 1967 from the shared vision of pioneering Indians to create a centre for social and cultural activities.

With the benevolence and magnanimity of His Highness Sheikh Zayed Bin Sultan Al Nahyan, the father of the U.A.E., the Unity Club transformed into India Social and Cultural Centre.

Today, ISC stands as a majestic, multi-story structure in the heart of Abu Dhabi, housing world-class facilities and serving as the primary hub for a community of over 10,000 members and their families.
